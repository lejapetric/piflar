Premo in krivo gibanje

Cela razlaga

1Premo gibanje

\(v = \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}\)
hitrost
100 m v 20 s: \(v = 5\ \text{m/s}\)
\(a = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}\)
pospešek
iz 0 na 20 m/s v 4 s: \(a = 5\ \text{m/s}^2\)
\(v = v_0 + at\)
enakomerno pospešeno
\(v_0 = 0, a = 2, t = 5\): \(v = 10\ \text{m/s}\)
\(s = v_0 t + \frac{at^2}{2}\)
pot
\(a = 2, t = 5\): \(s = 25\ \text{m}\)
\(v^2 = v_0^2 + 2as\)
brez časa
zavira z 20 m/s, \(a = -5\): \(s = 40\ \text{m}\)
ploščina pod \(v(t)\) = pot
naklon \(v(t)\) = pospešek
trikotnik pod \(v(t)\): \(s = \frac{vt}{2}\)

2Prosti pad in met

\(v = gt,\ h = \frac{gt^2}{2}\)
prosti pad, \(g = 9{,}8\ \text{m/s}^2\)
2 s: \(h \approx 20\ \text{m}\)
\(x = v_0 t,\ y = \frac{gt^2}{2}\)
vodoravni met
z višine 5 m: \(t = 1\ \text{s}\)

3Kroženje

\(v = \frac{2\pi r}{t_0} = \omega r\)
obodna hitrost
\(r = 2\ \text{m}, t_0 = 4\ \text{s}\): \(v = \pi\ \text{m/s}\)
\(\nu = \frac{1}{t_0},\ \omega = 2\pi\nu\)
frekvenca, kotna hitrost
\(t_0 = 0{,}5\ \text{s}\): \(\nu = 2\ \text{Hz}\)
\(a_r = \frac{v^2}{r} = \omega^2 r\)
radialni pospešek (proti središču)
\(v = 10, r = 50\): \(a_r = 2\ \text{m/s}^2\)

!Pazi

  • Pospešek pri zaviranju je negativen.
  • Pri kroženju s stalno hitrostjo je pospešek ≠ 0.
