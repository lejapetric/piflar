Delo in energija

Cela razlaga

1Delo

\(A = F s \cos\varphi\)
delo sile
\(F = 10\ \text{N}, s = 5\ \text{m}\): \(A = 50\ \text{J}\)
\(P = \frac{A}{t} = F v\)
moč
\(500\ \text{J}\) v \(10\ \text{s}\): \(50\ \text{W}\)
\(\eta = \frac{P_{kor}}{P_{vlož}}\)
izkoristek
80 W od 100 W: 80 %

2Energija

\(W_k = \frac{mv^2}{2}\)
kinetična
\(m = 2, v = 3\): \(9\ \text{J}\)
\(W_p = mgh\)
potencialna
\(m = 1, h = 10\): \(\approx 98\ \text{J}\)
\(W_{pr} = \frac{kx^2}{2}\)
prožnostna
\(k = 100, x = 0{,}1\): \(0{,}5\ \text{J}\)

3Ohranitev

\(A_{zun} = \Delta W_k + \Delta W_p + \Delta W_{pr}\)
izrek o mehanski energiji
brez zunanjih sil: \(W\) stalna
\(mgh = \frac{mv^2}{2}\)
pad z višine
\(h = 5\ \text{m}\): \(v \approx 10\ \text{m/s}\)

!Pazi

  • Delo sile pravokotno na premik je 0.
  • Trenje odvzema mehansko energijo.
