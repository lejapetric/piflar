Zaporedja in vrste

Cela razlaga

1Zaporedje

\(a_n = f(n),\ n \in \mathbb{N}\)
splošni člen
\(a_n = 2n - 1\): \(a_5 = 9\)
\(a_{n+1} - a_n > 0\)
narašča (\(< 0\): pada)
\(a_n = 3n + 1\): razlika \(3 > 0\), narašča
\(m \le a_n \le M\)
omejeno
\(a_n = \frac{1}{n}\): \(0 < a_n \le 1\)

2Aritmetično zaporedje

\(a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d\)
\(d = a_{n+1} - a_n\)
\(a_1 = 3,\ d = 4\): \(a_{10} = 3 + 9 \cdot 4 = 39\)
\(S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n)\)
vsota prvih \(n\) členov
\(1 + 3 + \ldots + 19\): \(S_{10} = 5 \cdot 20 = 100\)
\(a_n = \frac{a_{n-1} + a_{n+1}}{2}\)
srednji člen
\(x, 7, 11\): \(x = 2 \cdot 7 - 11 = 3\)

3Geometrijsko zaporedje

\(a_n = a_1 q^{n-1}\)
\(q = \frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\)
\(2, 6, 18, \ldots\): \(a_6 = 2 \cdot 3^5 = 486\)
\(S_n = a_1 \frac{q^n - 1}{q - 1}\)
\(q \ne 1\)
\(1 + 2 + \ldots + 2^9 = \frac{2^{10} - 1}{1} = 1023\)
\(a_n^2 = a_{n-1} a_{n+1}\)
srednji člen
\(4, x, 9\): \(x = \pm 6\)

4Limita zaporedja

\(|a_n - a| < \varepsilon\)
za skoraj vse \(n\)
\(a_n = \frac{2n+1}{n+3}\), \(\varepsilon = 0{,}01\): \(\frac{5}{n+3} < 0{,}01\), od \(n = 498\)
\(\lim \frac{1}{n} = 0,\ \lim q^n = 0\)
\(|q| < 1\)
\(\lim \frac{2n+1}{n+3} = \lim \frac{2 + \frac1n}{1 + \frac3n} = 2\)
\(\lim \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n = e\)
na listu formul
\(e \approx 2{,}718\)

5Vrste

\(s = \lim S_n\)
vsota vrste
konvergira, če limita obstaja
\(s = \frac{a_1}{1 - q}\)
samo za \(|q| < 1\)
\(8 + 4 + 2 + \ldots = \frac{8}{1 - \frac12} = 16\)
\(0{,}\overline{3} = \frac{0{,}3}{1 - 0{,}1}\)
periodično decimalno
\(= \frac{1}{3}\)

6Obrestni račun

\(G = G_0\left(1 + \frac{pt}{100}\right)\)
navadno obrestovanje
5000 €, 3 %, 4 leta: \(5000 \cdot 1{,}12 = 5600\) €
\(G_n = G_0 r^n,\ r = 1 + \frac{p}{100}\)
obrestno obrestovanje
\(5000 \cdot 1{,}03^4 = 5627{,}54\) €
\(r_r = 1 + \frac{p}{100m},\ r_k = \sqrt[m]{r}\)
relativna, konformna (\(m\) obdobij)
6 % mesečno: \(r_r = 1{,}005\), \(r_k = \sqrt[12]{1{,}06}\)
\(G_0 = \frac{G_n}{r^n}\)
diskontiranje (na danes)
2000 € čez 1 leto, 4 %: \(\frac{2000}{1{,}04} = 1923{,}08\) €

7Anuitete in amortizacija

\(a = D \frac{r^n (r - 1)}{r^n - 1}\)
iz \(D r^n = a \frac{r^n - 1}{r - 1}\)
\(D = 10\,000\), 6 %, 5 let: \(a = 2373{,}96\) €
\(o_k = D_{k-1} \frac{p}{100}\)
obresti od ostanka dolga
\(D = 3000\), 5 %: \(o_1 = 150\) €
\(R_k = a - o_k,\ D_k = D_{k-1} - R_k\)
razdolžnina, ostanek
\(a = 1101{,}63\): \(R_1 = 951{,}63\), \(D_1 = 2048{,}37\)

!Pazi

  • V \(a_n\) je \(n - 1\), ne \(n\).
  • Vsota vrste samo pri \(|q| < 1\).
  • Mesečno: leta pretvori v mesece.
  • Obresti od ostanka dolga, ne od začetnega.
