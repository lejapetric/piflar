1Zaporedje

\(a_n = f(n),\ n \in \mathbb{N}\)
splošni člen
\(a_n = 2n - 1\): \(a_5 = 9\)
\(a_{n+1} - a_n > 0\)
narašča (\(< 0\): pada)
\(a_n = 3n + 1\): razlika \(3 > 0\), narašča
\(m \le a_n \le M\)
omejeno
\(a_n = \frac{1}{n}\): \(0 < a_n \le 1\)

2Aritmetično zaporedje

\(a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d\)
\(d = a_{n+1} - a_n\)
\(a_1 = 3,\ d = 4\): \(a_{10} = 3 + 9 \cdot 4 = 39\)
\(S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n)\)
vsota prvih \(n\) členov
\(1 + 3 + \ldots + 19\): \(S_{10} = 5 \cdot 20 = 100\)
\(a_n = \frac{a_{n-1} + a_{n+1}}{2}\)
srednji člen
\(x, 7, 11\): \(x = 2 \cdot 7 - 11 = 3\)

3Geometrijsko zaporedje

\(a_n = a_1 q^{n-1}\)
\(q = \frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\)
\(2, 6, 18, \ldots\): \(a_6 = 2 \cdot 3^5 = 486\)
\(S_n = a_1 \frac{q^n - 1}{q - 1}\)
\(q \ne 1\)
\(1 + 2 + \ldots + 2^9 = \frac{2^{10} - 1}{1} = 1023\)
\(a_n^2 = a_{n-1} a_{n+1}\)
srednji člen
\(4, x, 9\): \(x = \pm 6\)

4Obrestni račun

\(G = G_0\left(1 + \frac{pt}{100}\right)\)
navadno obrestovanje
5000 €, 3 %, 4 leta: \(5000 \cdot 1{,}12 = 5600\) €
\(G_n = G_0 r^n,\ r = 1 + \frac{p}{100}\)
obrestno obrestovanje
\(5000 \cdot 1{,}03^4 = 5627{,}54\) €
\(r_r = 1 + \frac{p}{100m},\ r_k = \sqrt[m]{r}\)
relativna, konformna (\(m\) obdobij)
6 % mesečno: \(r_r = 1{,}005\), \(r_k = \sqrt[12]{1{,}06}\)
\(G_0 = \frac{G_n}{r^n}\)
diskontiranje (na danes)
2000 € čez 1 leto, 4 %: \(\frac{2000}{1{,}04} = 1923{,}08\) €
