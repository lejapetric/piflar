Členi, predlogi in odvisniki

Cela razlaga

1Členi

a / an
prvič omenjeno, ena od
I saw a dog.
the
znano, edinstveno
The dog was big. The sun.
brez člena
splošno, množina, neštevni
Dogs are loyal. Water is vital.

2Količinski izrazi

much / many
neštevni / števni
much time, many people
a little / a few
nekaj
a few friends, a little money
little / few
malo (negativno)
Few people came.
each, every, either, neither
determinanti
Neither answer is correct.

3Predlogi

at / on / in (čas)
ura / dan / mesec, leto
at 5, on Monday, in May
at / on / in (kraj)
točka / površina / notranjost
at the station, on the wall, in the box
depend on, interested in, good at
predložne vezave
She's good at maths.

4Oziralni odvisniki

who, which, that, whose, where
oziralni zaimki
The man who called is my uncle.
določilni / nedoločilni (z vejicami)
nedoločilni brez »that«
My sister, who lives in Rome, …
povezovalci
moreover, therefore, on the other hand
Therefore, we should act.

!Pazi

  • Nedoločilni odvisnik ima vejice in nikoli »that«.
