Členi, predlogi in odvisniki

V nalogah »gap fill (open)« moraš brez ponujenih možnosti vstaviti eno besedo. Najpogosteje manjka člen, predlog, oziralni zaimek, povezovalec ali pomožni glagol. Ta stran pokrije te majhne besede.

5Podpoglavja
2Tipi nalog
~10 minBranja

Kar moraš znati

  • a/an ob prvi omembi ali za poklic; the za znano, edinstveno ali že omenjeno
  • brez člena: množina in neštevni samostalniki v splošnem pomenu (Dogs are loyal. Water is vital.)
  • much/little z neštevnimi, many/few s števnimi; a few / a little = nekaj, few / little = malo
  • predlogi časa: at 5 o'clock, on Monday, in July / 2020 / the morning
  • who/which/that v določilnih odvisnikih; whose za svojino; where, when
  • v nedoločilnem odvisniku (z vejicami) ni that

Količinski izrazi in determinanti

Much, many, few, little, some, any, each, every.

Video razlaga

Razlaga

  • some v trdilnih povedih in ponudbah (Would you like some tea?), any v nikalnih in vprašalnih.
  • each (vsak posamezno, tudi dva) / every (vsi skupaj, tri ali več) + ednina.

Pogosti neštevni samostalniki: information, advice, news, furniture, equipment, luggage, homework, research, progress, knowledge. Ne dobijo a in nimajo množine: a piece of advice.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 1: Vrzel brez danih besed (open gap fill)

Naloga

Complete the text with ONE word in each gap: Last summer I went to (1) ___ island in Greece. (2) ___ the weather was hot, we spent most of the time (3) ___ the beach. The family (4) ___ house we rented was very kind.

Kako prepoznaš: navodilo pove, da vstaviš eno besedo, možnosti pa niso dane.

  • „ONE word“
  • „each gap“
  • „gap fill“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Preberi celo poved in določi besedno vrsto manjkajoče besede.

    1: pred samostalnikom; 2: na začetku odvisnika; 3: pred the beach; 4: pred samostalnikom, za osebo.

  2. Pri samostalniku pomisli na člen in determinante.

    1: prva omemba, samoglasnik → an.

  3. Pri dveh stavkih pomisli na veznik ali oziralni zaimek in razmerje (nasprotje, vzrok).

    2: vroče, zato na plaži → vzrok: As / Because / Since.

  4. Pri kraju in času uporabi preglednico predlogov; za svojino whose.

    3: on; 4: whose.

  5. Preberi besedilo z vstavljenimi besedami in preveri, da je vsaka beseda ena.

    an, As, on, whose.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: She is interested ___ photography.

in.

Primer 2: ___ the rain, the match went on.

Pred samostalnikom in z nasprotjem: Despite.

Ustaljene zveze in drobne besede s pol

Kaj vse lahko manjka v nalogi »Gap fill« z eno besedo.

Razlaga

Na vadbenih polah je manjkajoča beseda skoraj vedno ena od spodnjih skupin. Ko vrzel ne ustreza členu, predlogu časa in kraja ali oziralnemu zaimku (sklopi 1–4), preveri te sezname.

Glagol, pridevnik ali samostalnik + predlog (dodatno k sklopu 3)

ZvezaPrimer s pol
concentrate on, benefit from, respond to, warn against, turn into, struggle to + glagolLaughter soon turns into real laughter. Experts warn against relying too much on …
be associated with, be made from / of, be free of charge, take control of, get rid of, fall in love witha bottle made from seaweed; The museum is free of charge.
frazni glagoli s členicostay up / awake, get money back, keep invaders out, chase away, cover up, pass on to, turn out to be
agree / insist / claim that + stavekNot everyone agrees that a ban is the right answer.
be desperate / likely / the first to + glagol; used to + glagolmore likely to think about it; We used to buy …

Predlog by in ustaljene zveze

  • by + vršilec v trpniku (attacked by bandits), + način z -ing (by watching), + prevozno sredstvo (by air), + razlika v številkah (fell by more than 30 %, rose by almost fifty per cent)
  • parne zveze: side by side, piece by piece, from side to side, from village to village, from childhood to old age, over and over again, once or twice
  • stalne zveze: in advance, on a large scale, on the other hand, the other way round, for ever, for so long, no matter how, a matter of words, rich and poor alike, born of necessity
  • other than (razen), rather than (namesto), apart from, such as / like (za primer), a bit like

Primerjava

as … as (just as excited, as easily as, even something as simple as), twice as much, more … than, fewer than half, the same as, not … any longer / more, how + pridevnik (how valuable, how little we know).

Vezniki, ki jih sklop 4 ne omenja

Na začetku povedi z vejico: However, Nevertheless, Still, Instead, Besides. V povedi: unless (če ne), otherwise (sicer), whether (ali), neither … nor, both … and, not only … but also, until / till, once (ko enkrat), if / when, without / before / after + -ing ali samostalnik (without having to, an hour before bed), for + trajanje (for weeks, for thousands of years). Oziralno: which za cel stavek (…, which means that), in which / during which za predlogom.

Pazi: pri vsaki vrzeli preberi tudi besedo za njo. ___ of pogosto zahteva rid, matter, free, apart; ___ than zahteva primernik ali rather/other; vejica za vrzeljo na začetku povedi skoraj vedno pomeni However ali Instead.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 2: Vrzel, ki ni člen ne predlog časa ali kraja

Naloga

Write the missing word in each gap: Visits must be booked weeks (1) ___ advance. The number of visitors has fallen (2) ___ more than a third. The new products are more expensive. (3) ___, demand is growing quickly. One company sold (4) ___ as much as in the year before.

Kako prepoznaš: beseda pred vrzeljo ali za njo je del stalne zveze (advance, fallen … more than, as much as) ali pa je vrzel na začetku povedi pred vejico.

  • „one word missing in each gap“
  • vejica za vrzeljo
  • številke, primerjave
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Preberi besedo pred vrzeljo in za njo ter poišči stalno zvezo.

    1: ___ advance → in advance.

  2. Pri številkah in spremembah pomisli na by.

    2: fallen ___ more than a third → razlika → by.

  3. Vrzel na začetku povedi pred vejico: ugotovi razmerje s prejšnjo povedjo.

    3: dražje, a povpraševanje raste → nasprotje → However / Nevertheless / Still.

  4. Pri as … as in števnikih preveri množilnike.

    4: ___ as much as → twice.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: It took the islanders years getting ___ of the rats.

get rid of → rid.

Primer 2: The lake absorbs rainwater, ___ reduces the risk of floods.

Odnos do celega stavka → which.

Primer 3: Exotic animals cannot be happy in a flat, ___ matter how well they are cared for.

no matter how → no.

Ključ: kateri tip naloge je to?

Poišči besedo iz navodila naloge in skoči na pravi postopek.

Pogoste napake

  • Člen pred splošnim pojmom: The nature is beautiful → Nature is beautiful.
  • an informations / advices → some information / advice.
  • although pred samostalnikom: although the rain → despite the rain / although it rained.
  • that v nedoločilnem odvisniku: Ljubljana, that is the capital, → which.
  • in Monday, on 2019 → on Monday, in 2019.
