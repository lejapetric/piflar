Trpnik in poročani govor

Obe strukturi se na maturi pojavljata v nalogah s pretvorbami in vrzelmi, katalog pa izrecno omenja tudi pretvorbo premega govora v odvisnega. Pri obeh velja isto: ohraniš pomen, spremeniš obliko po strogih pravilih.

4Podpoglavja
3Tipi nalog
~10 minBranja

Kar moraš znati

  • trpnik = be v istem času kot v tvorniku + V3 (3. oblika glagola)
  • vršilca dodaš z by samo, če je pomemben
  • have/get something done: nekdo naredi nekaj zame
  • poročani govor: čas gre en korak nazaj (present → past, past → past perfect, will → would)
  • spremenijo se zaimki in časovni izrazi (today → that day, tomorrow → the next day)
  • poročano vprašanje ima besedni red trdilnega stavka in nima pomožnika do

4 · 1

Trpnik v vseh časih

Be + V3.

Razlaga

Trpnik uporabimo, ko je pomembnejše dejanje kot vršilec, ali ko vršilca ne poznamo: The bridge was built in 1901.

ČasTvornikTrpnik
present simpleThey clean the room.The room is cleaned.
present continuousThey are cleaning the room.The room is being cleaned.
present perfectThey have cleaned the room.The room has been cleaned.
past simpleThey cleaned the room.The room was cleaned.
past continuousThey were cleaning the room.The room was being cleaned.
past perfectThey had cleaned the room.The room had been cleaned.
futureThey will clean the room.The room will be cleaned.
modalniThey must clean the room.The room must be cleaned.
modalni v preteklostiThey should have cleaned it.It should have been cleaned.

Glagoli z dvema predmetoma

They gave me a prize. → I was given a prize. (pogosteje) ali A prize was given to me.

Neosebni trpnik (VR)

Za mnenja in poročila: People say that he is rich. → It is said that he is rich. ali He is said to be rich. Za preteklost: He is said to have left the country. Tako tudi believe, think, report, expect, know.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 1: Pretvori v trpnik

Naloga

Rewrite in the passive: Someone has stolen my bike.

Kako prepoznaš: zahteva passive ali začetek povedi s predmetom tvornika.

  • „passive“
  • „rewrite“
  • „begin with“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Poišči predmet tvornika; ta postane osebek.

    my bike.

  2. Določi čas glagola v tvorniku.

    has stolen → present perfect.

  3. Postavi be v isti čas in dodaj V3.

    has been stolen.

  4. Odloči, ali vršilec ostane (by …).

    someone izpustimo: My bike has been stolen.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: They are building a new school in our town.

A new school is being built in our town.

Primer 2: People believe that the painting is a fake. → The painting ___.

The painting is believed to be a fake.

4 · 2

Have something done

Ko dejanje opravi nekdo drug za nas.

Razlaga

have / get + predmet + V3: I had my hair cut. (frizer me je postrigel) — ne pa I cut my hair (sam sem se postrigel).

ČasPrimer
presentWe have our windows cleaned every month.
pastShe had her car repaired yesterday.
present perfectI've just had my phone fixed.
futureI'm going to have my eyes tested.

Ista struktura pove tudi, da se nam je zgodilo nekaj neprijetnega: He had his wallet stolen.

Pazi: Vrstni red: have + predmet + V3. I had cut my hair je past perfect in pomeni nekaj drugega.

Poročani govor

Premik časov, zaimki in časovni izrazi.

Razlaga

Ko poročamo, kaj je nekdo rekel, in je glagol poročanja v pretekliku (said, told), se čas premakne korak nazaj.

Premi govorPoročani govor
present simple: "I live here."past simple: He said he lived there.
present continuouspast continuous
present perfect / past simplepast perfect: She said she had seen it.
will / can / maywould / could / might
musthad to (ali must)
would, could, should, might, past perfectostanejo enaki
PremiPoročani
now, todaythen, that day
yesterdaythe day before, the previous day
tomorrowthe next day, the following day
last week / agothe week before / before
here, thisthere, that

say + (that) stavek; tell + oseba: He told me (that) …, nikoli He said me.

Pazi: Če je trditev še vedno resnična ali je glagol poročanja v sedanjiku (He says…), časa ni treba premakniti: She says she is tired.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 2: Pretvori premi govor v poročani

Naloga

Report: "I will phone you tomorrow," Tom said to me.

Kako prepoznaš: dan je stavek v narekovajih, naloga zahteva reported speech ali začetek He said / told.

  • „reported speech“
  • „report“
  • „said / told“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Izberi glagol poročanja in obliko.

    said to me → told me.

  2. Spremeni zaimke z vidika poročevalca.

    I → he, you → me.

  3. Premakni čas korak nazaj.

    will → would.

  4. Spremeni časovne in krajevne izraze.

    tomorrow → the next day: Tom told me he would phone me the next day.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: "I've lost my keys," she said.

She said (that) she had lost her keys.

Primer 2: "We can meet here," they said.

They said they could meet there.

4 · 4

Poročana vprašanja, ukazi in prošnje

Besedni red in glagoli poročanja.

Razlaga

Vprašanja

  • vprašanje z vprašalnico: "Where do you live?" → She asked me where I lived.
  • da/ne vprašanje: "Are you ready?" → He asked if/whether I was ready.
  • besedni red je kot v trdilnem stavku, brez do/does/did in brez vprašaja.

Ukazi in prošnje

tell / ask / order / advise + oseba + (not) to + V: "Close the door." → She told me to close the door.; "Don't be late." → He asked us not to be late.

Drugi glagoli poročanja

GlagolVzorecPrimer
suggest+ -ing / + that … shouldHe suggested going to the cinema.
offer, promise, refuse, agree+ to VShe promised to help.
admit, deny+ -ingHe denied taking the money.
apologisefor + -ingShe apologised for being late.
warnsb (not) to VThey warned us not to swim there.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 3: Poročaj vprašanje ali ukaz

Naloga

Report: "Did you enjoy the concert?" Ana asked me. / "Don't touch the screen," the teacher said to us.

Kako prepoznaš: v narekovajih je vprašanje (vprašaj) ali ukaz (velelnik).

  • „asked“
  • „told … to“
  • „reported question“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Prepoznaj vrsto stavka.

    Prvi: da/ne vprašanje. Drugi: nikalni ukaz.

  2. Vprašanje: asked + if/whether ali vprašalnica, nato trdilni besedni red s premaknjenim časom.

    Ana asked me if I had enjoyed the concert.

  3. Ukaz: told + oseba + (not) to + V.

    The teacher told us not to touch the screen.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: "What time does the train leave?" he asked.

He asked what time the train left.

Primer 2: "Let's take a taxi," she said.

She suggested taking a taxi.

Ključ: kateri tip naloge je to?

Poišči besedo iz navodila naloge in skoči na pravi postopek.

V navodilu naloge piše …Tip
„passive“, „rewrite“, „begin with“Tip 1 · Pretvori v trpnik
„reported speech“, „report“, „said / told“Tip 2 · Pretvori premi govor v poročani

Pogoste napake

  • Trpnik brez be: The house built in 1990 → The house was built in 1990.
  • He said me → He told me ali He said to me.
  • Vprašalni besedni red v poročanem vprašanju: She asked where did I live → where I lived.
  • Pozabljen premik časovnih izrazov: He said he would come tomorrow (če poročaš kasneje) → the next day.
  • suggest to go → suggest going ali suggest that we go.
