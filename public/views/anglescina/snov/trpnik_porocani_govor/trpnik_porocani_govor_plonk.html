Trpnik in poročani govor

Cela razlaga

1Trpnik

be + V3
v vseh časih
The house was built in 1900.
present perfect passive
has/have been + V3
The letter has been sent.
modal passive
modal + be + V3
It must be done today.
by + vršilec
le, če je pomemben
Hamlet was written by Shakespeare.

2Have something done

have/get + objekt + V3
nekdo drug naredi za nas
I had my hair cut.

3Poročani govor

pomik časov nazaj
present → past, past → past perfect
»I'm tired.« → He said he was tired.
will → would, can → could
modalni
She said she would come.
here → there, today → that day
pomik izrazov
tomorrow → the next day
say / tell somebody
»tell« zahteva osebo
He told me that …

4Vprašanja, ukazi, prošnje

if / whether + trdilni red
da/ne vprašanje
She asked if I was ready.
vprašalnica + trdilni red
vprašalnica
He asked where I lived.
tell / ask + to
ukaz, prošnja
She told me to sit down.

!Pazi

  • V poročanem vprašanju ni pomožnika »do«.
