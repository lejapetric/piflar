Pridevniki, prislovi in primerjava

Pridevnik opisuje samostalnik (a careful driver), prislov pa glagol, pridevnik ali celo poved (she drives carefully). Na maturi se ta snov pojavi v vrzelih pole 1B, pri besedotvorju in v pisnih sestavkih, kjer s primerjavami in natančnimi prislovi pokažeš bogatejši jezik.

3Podpoglavja
4Tipi nalog
~12 minBranja

Kar moraš znati

  • kratki pridevniki: -er / the -est (cheaper, the cheapest); dolgi: more / the most (more expensive)
  • nepravilni: good – better – best, bad – worse – worst, far – further – furthest, little – less – least, much/many – more – most
  • enakost: as … as, neenakost: not as/so … as; vzporedna rast: the more …, the better
  • okrepitev primernika: much, far, a lot, even, a bit, slightly (nikoli very + primernik)
  • prislov iz pridevnika: -ly; izjeme good → well, hard → hard, fast → fast, late → late; hardly, lately pomenita nekaj drugega
  • too + pridevnik (preveč), pridevnik + enough (dovolj); so + pridevnik, such (a) + pridevnik + samostalnik

Formula: pridevnik ob samostalniku ali za be, look, seem, feel, sound, taste; prislov ob vseh drugih glagolih.

Further ali farther, elder ali older, less ali fewer

  • farther samo za razdaljo; further za razdaljo in »dodaten« (further information).
  • elder, eldest samo pred samostalnikom za družinske člane (my elder sister); sicer older (She is older than me).
  • less z neštevnimi (less sugar), fewer s števnimi v množini (fewer cars).

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 1: Vstavi pravilno stopnjo pridevnika ali prislova

Naloga

Complete with the correct form of the word in brackets: Mount Everest is ___ (high) mountain in the world, but K2 is ___ (difficult) to climb.

Kako prepoznaš: v oklepaju je pridevnik ali prislov v osnovni obliki, v povedi so than, the, in the world, of all, as.

  • „correct form“
  • „than“
  • „the … in / of“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Poišči signal: than → primernik, the … in/of/ever → presežnik, as … as → osnovna oblika.

    Prva vrzel: in the world → presežnik. Druga vrzel: primerjava dveh gora brez than v besedilu, a pomen je »težja« → primernik.

  2. Preštej zloge in preveri, ali je beseda nepravilna.

    high: en zlog → -est. difficult: trije zlogi → more.

  3. Dodaj the pred presežnik in preveri pravopis (podvojitev, y → i).

    the highest, more difficult.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: This is ___ (bad) film I have ever seen.

ever → presežnik, nepravilni: the worst.

Primer 2: Could you speak a bit ___ (slowly)?

Prislov na -ly → more slowly.

Tip 2: Pretvorba s primerjalno zvezo

Naloga

Complete the second sentence so that it means the same: Tom is taller than Ben. → Ben isn't ___ Tom.

Kako prepoznaš: dana je poved s primerjavo in začetek nove, ki mora pomeniti isto.

  • „means the same“
  • „as … as“
  • „the more“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Ugotovi, kdo je »več« in kdo »manj«.

    Tom je višji, Ben je nižji.

  2. Izberi zvezo, ki jo nakazuje začetek: not as … as, the … the, presežnik z ever, nobody … as.

    Ben isn't → not as … as.

  3. Pridevnik v zvezi as … as je v osnovni obliki.

    Ben isn't as tall as Tom.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: I have never read such a boring book. → It's the ___.

It's the most boring book I have ever read.

Primer 2: If you train harder, you will become stronger. → The ___.

The harder you train, the stronger you will become.

Prislovi in vrstni red pridevnikov

Pridevnik ali prislov, tvorba prislovov, mesto v povedi.

Razlaga

Pridevnik ali prislov?

Vprašaj se, kaj beseda opisuje. Samostalnik → pridevnik: She is a quiet girl. Glagol → prislov: She speaks quietly. Za glagoli stanja in zaznavanja (be, become, look, seem, appear, feel, smell, sound, taste, get) stoji pridevnik: The soup tastes good. You look tired.

PridevnikPrislovPozor
careful, quick, quietcarefully, quickly, quietlyosnovno pravilo: -ly
easy, happyeasily, happilyy → i
terrible, gentleterribly, gently-le → -ly
basic, dramaticbasically, dramatically-ic → -ically (izjema publicly)
goodwellwell je tudi pridevnik »zdrav«: I don't feel well.
hard, fast, late, early, highhard, fast, late, early, highenaka oblika
friendly, lovely, lonely, sillyin a friendly waypridevniki na -ly nimajo prislova
Pazi: hardly = komaj (I hardly know him), lately = v zadnjem času, nearly = skoraj, highly = zelo (highly recommended). He works hard ≠ He hardly works.

Mesto prislovov

  • pogostnost (always, usually, often, sometimes, never): pred glavnim glagolom, za be in za pomožnim glagolom: She often goes out. He is never late. I have always liked it.
  • način – kraj – čas na koncu povedi v tem vrstnem redu: She sang beautifully at the concert last night.
  • prislov ne stoji med glagolom in predmetom: He speaks English well (ne speaks well English).
  • still, already, just pred glavnim glagolom; yet na koncu nikalnic in vprašanj.

Vrstni red pridevnikov

Ko pred samostalnikom stoji več pridevnikov, je vrstni red mnenje – velikost – starost – oblika – barva – izvor – snov – namen + samostalnik. Pomnilo: OSASCOMP (opinion, size, age, shape, colour, origin, material, purpose).

a lovely small old round wooden table; a beautiful long red silk evening dress; an expensive Italian leather bag

Pridevniki na -ed in -ing

-ed opisuje, kako se počuti oseba (I was bored), -ing pa, kakšna je stvar, ki ta občutek povzroča (The lecture was boring). Podrobneje v Besedotvorju.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 3: Pridevnik ali prislov, vrstni red

Naloga

Choose the correct word: The new manager seems very (polite / politely), but she speaks (quick / quickly).

Kako prepoznaš: izbiraš med obliko z -ly in brez nje ali urejaš vrstni red besed.

  • „choose the correct word“
  • „put the words in order“
  • „adjective or adverb“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Poišči besedo, ki jo vrzel opisuje.

    Prva: seems (glagol stanja) → opisuje osebo. Druga: speaks → opisuje dejanje.

  2. Glagol stanja ali samostalnik → pridevnik; vsak drug glagol → prislov.

    polite, quickly.

  3. Pri več pridevnikih uredi po OSASCOMP.

    The new manager seems very polite, but she speaks quickly.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: The flowers smell (lovely / lovelily).

smell je glagol zaznavanja → lovely (lovelily ne obstaja).

Primer 2: leather / black / old / a / jacket

starost – barva – snov: an old black leather jacket.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 4: Pretvorba s too, enough, so ali such

Naloga

Complete the second sentence so that it means the same: The coffee was so hot that I couldn't drink it. → The coffee was too ___.

Kako prepoznaš: poved ima posledico z that ali to, nova poved se začne s too, enough, so, such.

  • „too“
  • „enough“
  • „so … that / such … that“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Ugotovi, ali je posledica pozitivna (lahko) ali negativna (ne morem).

    couldn't drink → negativna.

  2. Negativna posledica: too + pridevnik + to, ali not + nasprotni pridevnik + enough to. Pozitivna: pridevnik + enough to.

    too hot to drink.

  3. Izpusti predmet, ki je že osebek (it).

    The coffee was too hot (for me) to drink.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: He is too young to drive. → He isn't ___.

He isn't old enough to drive.

Primer 2: The film was so exciting that nobody left. → It was such ___.

It was such an exciting film that nobody left.

Ključ: kateri tip naloge je to?

Poišči besedo iz navodila naloge in skoči na pravi postopek.

V navodilu ali povedi piše …Tip
„correct form“, „than“, „the … in / of / ever“Tip 1 · Vstavi pravilno stopnjo
„means the same“, „as … as“, „The more …“Tip 2 · Pretvorba s primerjalno zvezo
izbira med -ly in brez, „put the words in order“Tip 3 · Pridevnik ali prislov, vrstni red

Pogoste napake

  • Dvojni primernik: more cheaper → cheaper; the most best → the best.
  • than in then: bigger then → bigger than.
  • very pred primernikom: very better → much better.
  • Prislov za glagolom stanja: It sounds well → It sounds good; I feel badly → I feel bad.
  • hardly namesto hard: She studied hardly pomeni »komaj se je učila«.
  • enough pred pridevnikom: enough old → old enough.
  • such brez člena pred števnim samostalnikom v ednini: such nice day → such a nice day.
