Modalni glagoli, nedoločnik in -ing

Cela razlaga

1Sposobnost, dovoljenje, dolžnost

can / could / be able to
sposobnost
I could swim at five.
may / can / be allowed to
dovoljenje
You may leave.
must / have to
dolžnost (notranja / zunanja)
I must study. I have to wear a uniform.
mustn't / don't have to
prepoved / ni treba
You mustn't smoke. You don't have to come.
should / ought to
nasvet
You should see a doctor.

2Verjetnost in sklepanje

must / can't + infinitive
gotovo / nemogoče (sedanjost)
He must be tired.
must / can't have + V3
sklepanje o preteklosti
She can't have seen us.
might / could / may (have + V3)
možnost
He might have missed the bus.
should have + V3
kritika preteklosti
You should have told me.

3-ing ali to + nedoločnik

enjoy, avoid, mind, suggest + -ing
glagoli z -ing
I enjoy reading.
want, decide, hope, refuse + to
glagoli z nedoločnikom
She decided to leave.
remember / stop / try + -ing / to
sprememba pomena
I stopped smoking. / I stopped to smoke.

!Pazi

  • Po modalnem glagolu nedoločnik brez »to«: He can swim.
