Modalni glagoli, nedoločnik in -ing

Modalni glagoli povedo, kako govorec gleda na dejanje: ali je mogoče, nujno, dovoljeno ali verjetno. Na maturi se pojavljajo v vrzelih in pretvorbah, skupaj z vprašanjem, ali za glagolom pride to + nedoločnik ali oblika na -ing.

3Podpoglavja
3Tipi nalog
~10 minBranja

Kar moraš znati

  • za modalnim glagolom vedno osnovna oblika brez to (must go, ne must to go)
  • must (notranja nujnost, sklep) / have to (zunanja obveznost); mustn't (prepovedano) / don't have to (ni treba)
  • verjetnost: must (skoraj gotovo), may/might/could (mogoče), can't (skoraj gotovo ne)
  • sklep o preteklosti: modalni glagol + have + V3 (must have forgotten)
  • should have + V3 = očitek ali obžalovanje (bilo bi prav, pa nisi)
  • -ing za enjoy, avoid, mind, finish, suggest; to + V za want, decide, hope, refuse, manage

Podpoglavja

  1. 1 Sposobnost, dovoljenje, dolžnost, nasvet
  2. 2 Verjetnost in sklepanje
  3. 3 Glagol + -ing ali to + nedoločnik
  4. ? Ključ: kateri tip naloge je to?

Sposobnost, dovoljenje, dolžnost, nasvet

Pomen modalnih glagolov v sedanjosti.

Razlaga

PomenSedanjostPreteklost / drugi časi
sposobnostcan, be able tocould (splošno), was able to / managed to (enkraten uspeh), will be able to
dovoljenjecan, may (formalno), could (vljudno)was allowed to
obveznostmust, have tohad to, will have to

prepovedmustn't, can'twasn't allowed to

Must ali have to

I must study tonight. (sam se odločim) — I have to wear a uniform. (pravilo šole). V preteklosti je za obe le had to.

Pazi: mustn't in don't have to nista sopomenki: You mustn't tell anyone (prepovedano) proti You don't have to come (ni nujno, lahko pa prideš).

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 1: Izberi modalni glagol po pomenu

Naloga

Choose the correct option: You (mustn't / don't have to) bring food, lunch is included. But you (mustn't / don't have to) be late, the bus leaves at 8.

Kako prepoznaš: ponujenih je več modalnih glagolov, ki se razlikujejo po pomenu.

  • „choose“
  • „circle“
  • „must / have to / should“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Za vsako vrzel prevedi stavek v slovenščino s »ni treba«, »ne smeš«, »moraš«, »lahko«.

    Prva: hrane ti ni treba prinesti. Druga: ne smeš zamuditi.

  2. Poveži pomen z glagolom iz preglednice.

    ni treba → don't have to; ne smeš → mustn't.

  3. Preveri čas.

    Oboje je sedanjost.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: When I was five, I ___ swim. (can)

Splošna sposobnost v preteklosti: could.

Primer 2: The fire spread fast, but everyone ___ escape. (could / was able to)

Enkraten uspeh: was able to.

Verjetnost in sklepanje

Kako gotovo je nekaj res, zdaj in v preteklosti.

Razlaga

GotovostO sedanjostiO preteklosti
skoraj gotovo damust bemust have been
mogočemay / might / could bemay / might / could have been
skoraj gotovo necan't / couldn't becan't / couldn't have been

She isn't answering. She must be asleep. — He can't have seen us; he didn't say hello.

Drugi pomeni s perfektom

  • should have / ought to have + V3: bilo bi prav, a se ni zgodilo (You should have told me.)
  • needn't have + V3: naredil si, a ni bilo treba (You needn't have bought flowers.)
  • could have + V3: možnost, ki je nisi izkoristil (You could have asked me for help.)

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 2: Sklep o preteklosti

Naloga

Complete: The streets are wet. It ___ (rain) during the night.

Kako prepoznaš: iz dokaza (zdaj) sklepaš, kaj se je zgodilo prej; v nalogi so namigi kot I'm sure, perhaps, it's impossible.

  • „must have“
  • „can't have“
  • „I'm sure / perhaps“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Ugotovi, ali gre za sedanjost ali preteklost.

    Dež je bil ponoči → preteklost.

  2. Oceni stopnjo gotovosti.

    Mokre ulice → skoraj gotovo.

  3. Zapiši modalni glagol + have + V3.

    must have rained.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: I'm sure he didn't steal it. → He ___.

He can't have stolen it.

Primer 2: It was a mistake not to book a table. → We ___.

We should have booked a table.

Glagol + -ing ali to + nedoločnik

Katera oblika sledi glagolu.

Razlaga

VzorecGlagoli
+ -ingenjoy, avoid, mind, finish, suggest, consider, deny, admit, risk, miss, can't stand, keep, give up, look forward to, be used to
+ to Vwant, decide, hope, plan, agree, refuse, promise, manage, afford, offer, expect, seem, learn, would like
+ oseba + to Vwant sb to, ask, tell, advise, allow, encourage, expect, remind, warn
+ oseba + V (brez to)make sb do, let sb do, help sb (to) do

Spremenjen pomen

Glagol+ -ing+ to V
remember / forgetspomniti se nečesa, kar si že naredilspomniti se, da moraš nekaj narediti
stopnehati z dejanjemustaviti se, da bi nekaj naredil
tryposkusiti (kot preizkus)truditi se
regretobžalovati pretekloz obžalovanjem sporočiti

Za predlogom je vedno -ing: interested in learning, good at swimming, instead of waiting, before leaving.

Pazi: used to + V = navada v preteklosti (I used to play football), be/get used to + -ing = biti/navaditi se (I'm used to getting up early).

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 3: Vstavi -ing ali to + nedoločnik

Naloga

Complete: I really enjoy ___ (cook), but I can't afford ___ (eat) out every day. Remember ___ (buy) milk on your way home!

Kako prepoznaš: za vrzeljo je glagol v osnovni obliki, pred vrzeljo drug glagol, pridevnik ali predlog.

  • „correct form“
  • „-ing or to“
  • „verbs in brackets“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Poglej besedo tik pred vrzeljo.

    enjoy, afford, remember.

  2. Če je predlog, uporabi -ing.

    Ni predloga.

  3. Poišči glagol v preglednici; pri remember, stop, try odloči po pomenu.

    enjoy + -ing, afford + to V; remember (nekaj, kar moraš še narediti) + to V.

  4. Zapiši.

    cooking, to eat, to buy.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: She's looking forward to ___ (see) you.

to je tu predlog: seeing.

Primer 2: My parents made me ___ (apologise).

make + oseba + V brez to: apologise.

Ključ: kateri tip naloge je to?

Poišči besedo iz navodila naloge in skoči na pravi postopek.

V navodilu naloge piše …Tip

Pogoste napake

  • to za modalnim glagolom: I can to swim → I can swim.
  • mustn't namesto don't have to, ko nekaj ni obvezno.
  • must v preteklosti: Yesterday I must work → I had to work.
  • He suggested me to go → He suggested that I go / He suggested going.
  • look forward to see → look forward to seeing.
