Pregled slovnice za maturo

Vsa slovnica, ki jo preverjajo pole in jo potrebuješ za sestavke, na eni strani. Ob vsakem pravilu je kratek primer, oznaka ravni in povezava na temo s celotno razlago in nalogami. Globina sledi učbenikom ravni B2, ki jih šole uporabljajo za pripravo na maturo. Zgradbe, ki so samo za višjo raven, so razložene v zadnjem sklopu.

Kako uporabiš pregled

  • pred maturo preberi vsa pravila; pri vsakem, kjer ne znaš razložiti primera, klikni na podrobno temo
  • pravila brez oznake potrebuješ na obeh ravneh
  • VR: pravilo je značilno za višjo raven, na osnovni pa ti prinese dodatne točke pri sestavkih
  • v vsak sestavek vključi vsaj pet različnih zgradb s seznama
  • naloge v poli 1B: izbirni tip, odprte vrzeli, glagoli v oklepaju, besedotvorje

Tip 1: Glagol v oklepaju (gap fill, verbs)

Kako prepoznaš: pola 1B, besedilo z glagoli v oklepaju; vpisati moraš pravo obliko, včasih tudi nikalno ali trpno.

Univerzalni postopek

Zgradbe za višjo raven

Poudarek, krajšanje in pogled iz preteklosti.

Razlaga

1. Inverzija po nikalnih in omejevalnih izrazih

Ko poved začneš z nikalnim ali omejevalnim izrazom, zamenjaš osebek in pomožni glagol kot v vprašanju. Zgradba je formalna in poudari povedano.

IzrazZgradbaPrimer
Never, Rarely, Seldom+ have/do + osebekNever have I seen such a crowd. Rarely do we agree.
Not only … but alsoNot only + do/does/did + osebekNot only does it save money, but it also protects nature.
Hardly / Scarcely … when; No sooner … than+ had + osebek + 3. oblikaNo sooner had we sat down than the phone rang.
Only then, Only when, Only afterinverzija v glavnem stavkuOnly when I left home did I appreciate my parents.
Under no circumstances, At no time, In no way+ pomožni glagol + osebekUnder no circumstances should you open the door.
Little+ did + osebekLittle did she know what was waiting for her.
Pazi: če v povedi ni pomožnega glagola, dodaš do/does/did: I rarely eat meat → Rarely do I eat meat. Pri Only when je inverzija šele v drugem (glavnem) stavku.

2. Cleft sentences (razcepljene povedi)

Z njimi poudariš en del povedi. Iz The ending surprised me nastane:

  • It is/was + poudarjeni del + that/who: It was the ending that surprised me. It was Ana who called.
  • What + stavek + is/was: What surprised me was the ending. What I need is a break.
  • All (that) … is: All I want is some peace.
  • The reason why … is that, The thing that …: The reason why I left is that I was tired.

3. Poudarjeni do

V trdilni povedi dodaš do/does/did pred osnovno obliko, kadar nasprotuješ ali poudariš: I do understand your point, but…, She did call you, you just didn't hear. V govoru je do naglašen.

4. Deležijski stavki (participle clauses)

Odvisnik skrajšaš, kadar ima isti osebek kot glavni stavek.

OblikaPomenPrimer
-ingsočasno dejanje ali vzrok (aktivno)Feeling tired, she went to bed. (= Because she felt tired…)
Having + 3. oblikaprej končano dejanjeHaving finished the test, he left. (= After he had finished…)
3. oblikatrpni pomenWritten in 1945, the novel is still popular. (= which was written…)
za samostalnikomskrajšan oziralni stavekThe man standing at the door is my uncle. Products made in China…
Pazi: osebek deležijskega stavka mora biti osebek glavnega stavka. Walking home, the rain started je napaka (dež ni hodil domov) → Walking home, I got caught in the rain.

5. Prihodnost v preteklosti

Ko o prihodnosti govoriš z vidika preteklosti, se will spremeni v would, am going to v was going to:

  • She knew she would never return. (takrat še prihodnost)
  • I was going to call you, but I forgot. (načrt, ki se ni uresničil)
  • We were about to leave when it started to snow. (tik pred tem)
  • The train was to leave at six. (formalno, dogovor)

Pregled vseh zahtev višje ravni je v temi Višja raven: vse zahteve.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 2: Preoblikuj poved s poudarkom

Naloga

Rewrite the sentence starting with the words given: I had never seen such a beautiful place. → Never …

Kako prepoznaš: poved moraš začeti z danim izrazom (Never, Not only, What, It was, Having), pomen ostane enak.

  • „starting with“
  • „means the same“
  • „Never / Not only / What“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Ugotovi, katero zgradbo zahteva dani začetek.

    Never na začetku → inverzija

  2. Poišči pomožni glagol (ali dodaj do/does/did) in ga postavi pred osebek.

    I had seen → had I seen

  3. Prepiši preostanek povedi in preveri, da se pomen ni spremenil.

    Never had I seen such a beautiful place.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: The noise annoyed me most. → What …

What annoyed me most was the noise.

Primer 2: After she had read the letter, she burst into tears. → Having …

Having read the letter, she burst into tears.

Pogoste napake

  • Present perfect z znanim časom: I have seen him yesterday → I saw him yesterday.
  • will za if in when: If it will rain → If it rains.
  • Manjkajoči -s v 3. osebi ednine: She work → She works.
  • Členi: The life is beautiful → Life is beautiful; I play the football → I play football.
  • more better, the most easiest: dvojna primerjava.
  • Despite of, Although…, but…
  • Inverzija brez pomožnega glagola: Never I have seen → Never have I seen.
  • Deležijski stavek z drugim osebkom: Walking home, the rain started.
