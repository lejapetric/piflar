Pregled slovnice za maturo

Cela razlaga

1Mreža časov

present simple
navade, dejstva
She works in a bank.
present continuous
zdaj, začasno
I'm reading now.
past simple
končano v preteklosti (čas znan)
I saw him yesterday.
past continuous
dogajanje v teku v preteklosti
I was cooking when he called.
present perfect
izkušnja, povezava s sedanjostjo
I have been to London.
present perfect continuous
traja do zdaj
I've been waiting for an hour.
past perfect
pred drugim preteklim dejanjem
The train had left when we arrived.

2Present perfect ali past simple

ever, never, just, already, yet, since, for
present perfect
Have you ever met him?
yesterday, ago, in 2020, last week
past simple
I met him two years ago.

3Prihodnost

will
odločitev zdaj, napoved
I'll help you.
going to
namen, napoved z dokazom
Look at those clouds, it's going to rain.
present continuous
dogovor
I'm meeting Tom at 6.
future perfect / continuous
do / ob času v prihodnosti
By 2030 I will have finished.

4Štirje pogojniki

0: if + present, present
splošna resnica
If you heat ice, it melts.
1: if + present, will
realna prihodnost
If it rains, I'll stay home.
2: if + past, would
neresnično zdaj
If I had money, I would travel.
3: if + past perfect, would have + V3
neresnična preteklost
If I had studied, I would have passed.

5Trpnik

be + V3
v vseh časih
The house was built in 1900.
present perfect passive
has/have been + V3
The letter has been sent.
modal passive
modal + be + V3
It must be done today.
by + vršilec
le, če je pomemben
Hamlet was written by Shakespeare.

6Poročani govor

pomik časov nazaj
present → past, past → past perfect
»I'm tired.« → He said he was tired.
will → would, can → could
modalni
She said she would come.
here → there, today → that day
pomik izrazov
tomorrow → the next day
say / tell somebody
»tell« zahteva osebo
He told me that …

7Sposobnost, dovoljenje, dolžnost

can / could / be able to
sposobnost
I could swim at five.
may / can / be allowed to
dovoljenje
You may leave.
must / have to
dolžnost (notranja / zunanja)
I must study. I have to wear a uniform.
mustn't / don't have to
prepoved / ni treba
You mustn't smoke. You don't have to come.
should / ought to
nasvet
You should see a doctor.

8-ing ali to + nedoločnik

enjoy, avoid, mind, suggest + -ing
glagoli z -ing
I enjoy reading.
want, decide, hope, refuse + to
glagoli z nedoločnikom
She decided to leave.
remember / stop / try + -ing / to
sprememba pomena
I stopped smoking. / I stopped to smoke.

9Primerjava

-er / -est
kratki pridevniki
tall, taller, the tallest
more / most
dolgi
more interesting
good, better, best; bad, worse, worst
nepravilni
far, further, furthest
as … as / not as … as
enako / manj
He's as tall as me.
the + comp., the + comp.
čim bolj, tem bolj
The sooner, the better.

10Oziralni odvisniki

who, which, that, whose, where
oziralni zaimki
The man who called is my uncle.
določilni / nedoločilni (z vejicami)
nedoločilni brez »that«
My sister, who lives in Rome, …
povezovalci
moreover, therefore, on the other hand
Therefore, we should act.

11Višja raven

inverzija
po negativnem prislovu
Never have I seen such a mess.
cleft sentences
poudarek
What I need is a holiday.

!Pazi

  • Po »if« ni »will«.
