Vprašanja, vprašalni dodatki in zaimki

Pravilno vprašanje potrebuješ pri ustnem izpitu, v e-sporočilih in pismih (Could you tell me…?) ter pri vrzelih v poli 1B, kjer pogosto manjka pomožni glagol ali zaimek. Na tej strani so tvorba vprašanj, vprašalni dodatki, posredna vprašanja in povratni zaimki.

3Podpoglavja
4Tipi nalog
~11 minBranja

Kar moraš znati

  • vprašanje: (vprašalnica) + pomožni glagol + osebek + glavni glagol: Where do you live?
  • vprašanje po osebku nima do/does/did: Who broke the window?
  • question tag: trdilna poved → nikalni dodatek in obratno; isti pomožni glagol in čas: You're coming, aren't you?
  • posebni dodatki: I am → aren't I, Let's → shall we, ukaz → will you, nobody/everyone → they
  • posredno vprašanje ima besedni red trdilne povedi: Can you tell me where the station is?; da/ne vprašanje z if / whether
  • povratni zaimki: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves; vzajemno: each other

Formula: v pravem vprašanju je pomožni glagol pred osebkom, v posrednem pa za njim.

Tvorba vprašanj

Pomožni glagol, vprašalnice in vprašanja po osebku.

Razlaga

Angleško vprašanje vedno potrebuje pomožni glagol pred osebkom. Če ga v trdilni povedi ni, ga dodaš (do, does, did), glavni glagol pa je potem v osnovni obliki.

Trdilna povedVprašanjePravilo
She is reading.Is she reading?be zamenja mesto z osebkom
They have finished.Have they finished?pomožni have
He can swim.Can he swim?modalni glagol
You live here.Do you live here?present simple: do/does
She works in Koper.Where does she work?does + osnovna oblika (brez -s)
They went home.When did they go home?did + osnovna oblika

Vprašanje po osebku ali po predmetu

Ko vprašalnica (who, what, which, whose, how many) sprašuje po osebku, je besedni red kot v trdilni povedi in do/does/did ni:

  • Who called you? (nekdo te je poklical; sprašujemo po osebku)
  • Who did you call? (ti si nekoga poklical; sprašujemo po predmetu)
  • What happened? (nikoli What did happen?)
  • How many students passed the exam?

Predlog na koncu

V pogovorni angleščini predlog ostane na koncu: Who are you talking to? What is this book about? Where do you come from? V formalnem slogu: To whom…?

Vprašalnice

VprašalnicaSprašuje poPrimer
how long / how often / how fartrajanju, pogostosti, razdaljiHow long have you lived here?
how much / how manykoličini (neštevno / števno)How many tickets did you buy?

Pazi: What is he like? (kakšen je po značaju) ≠ What does he like? (kaj ima rad) ≠ How is he? (kako se počuti).

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 1: Postavi vprašanje za podčrtani del

Naloga

Ask a question about the underlined part: Sarah bought three books yesterday. / Sarah bought three books yesterday.

Kako prepoznaš: del povedi je podčrtan ali je v navodilu, po čem sprašuješ.

  • „ask a question“
  • „underlined“
  • „question word“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Izberi vprašalnico za podčrtani del.

    three → How many books; Sarah → Who.

  2. Ali je podčrtani del osebek? Če je, ostane besedni red trdilne povedi.

    Sarah je osebek → Who bought three books yesterday?

  3. Sicer dodaj pomožni glagol v pravem času, glavni glagol v osnovni obliki.

    bought → did … buy: How many books did Sarah buy yesterday?

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: They have lived here for ten years.

How long have they lived here?

Primer 2: The storm damaged the roof.

Osebek: What damaged the roof?

Vprašalni dodatki in posredna vprašanja

Question tags ter vljudna vprašanja s Could you tell me…

Razlaga

Vprašalni dodatki (question tags)

Dodatek na koncu povedi pomeni »kajne?«. Sestavljen je iz pomožnega glagola iz povedi in osebnega zaimka. Trdilna poved dobi nikalni dodatek, nikalna pa trdilnega.

PovedDodatekPravilo
She is Slovenian,isn't she?isti pomožni glagol
You don't eat meat,do you?nikalna → trdilni
He plays the guitar,doesn't he?present simple → do/does
They missed the train,didn't they?past simple → did
You've seen it,haven't you?'ve = have
He'd left,hadn't he?'d + V3 = had
She'd like tea,wouldn't she?'d + osnovna oblika = would
I'm late,aren't I?izjema
Let's go,shall we?predlog
Close the door,will you?ukaz
Nobody called,did they?nobody, nothing, never so nikalni → trdilni dodatek; everybody/somebody → they
There's a problem,isn't there?there ostane

Posredna vprašanja

Vljudno vprašanje začnemo z uvodom (Could you tell me…, Do you know…, I wonder…, I'd like to know…). Nato je besedni red kot v trdilni povedi: osebek + glagol, brez do/does/did.

NeposrednoPosredno
Where is the museum?Could you tell me where the museum is?
What time does the shop open?Do you know what time the shop opens?
Why did she leave?I wonder why she left.
Is breakfast included?I'd like to know if/whether breakfast is included.
Pazi: v formalnem e-sporočilu ali pismu (pola 3A) so posredna vprašanja najbolj vljudna izbira: I would be grateful if you could tell me whether…. Če uvod ni vprašanje (I wonder…), na koncu ni vprašaja.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 2: Dopolni z vprašalnim dodatkom

Naloga

Add a question tag: Your brother has never been to London, ___?

Kako prepoznaš: poved se konča z vejico in vrzeljo pred vprašajem.

  • „question tag“
  • „, ___?“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Poišči pomožni glagol (ali ga dodaj: do/does/did).

    has … been → has.

  2. Ugotovi, ali je poved nikalna (tudi z never, nobody, hardly).

    never → nikalna → trdilni dodatek.

  3. Osebek zamenjaj z osebnim zaimkom.

    your brother → he: …, has he?

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: I'm right, ___?

aren't I?

Primer 2: Everyone enjoyed the trip, ___?

didn't they?

Tip 3: Spremeni v posredno vprašanje

Naloga

Make the question more polite: When does the next train leave? → Could you tell me ___?

Kako prepoznaš: dano je neposredno vprašanje in vljuden uvod.

  • „Could you tell me“
  • „Do you know“
  • „I wonder“
  • „more polite“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Prepiši vprašalnico (ali dodaj if/whether pri da/ne vprašanju).

    when.

  2. Odstrani do/does/did in prenesi čas na glavni glagol.

    does … leave → leaves.

  3. Postavi osebek pred glagol.

    Could you tell me when the next train leaves?

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: Is the museum open on Mondays? → Do you know ___?

Do you know if the museum is open on Mondays?

Primer 2: How much did the tickets cost? → I wonder ___.

I wonder how much the tickets cost.

Povratni, poudarjalni in drugi zaimki

Myself, each other, by myself, whoever, whatever.

Razlaga

OsebniPredmetniSvojilniPovratni
Imemy / minemyself
youyouyour / yoursyourself / yourselves
he / she / ithim / her / ithis / her(s) / itshimself / herself / itself
weusour / oursourselves
theythemtheir / theirsthemselves

Raba povratnih zaimkov

  • povratno: osebek in predmet sta ista oseba: She cut herself. Enjoy yourselves! Help yourself.
  • poudarjalno: »sam, osebno«: I painted the room myself. The manager himself apologised.
  • by + povratni = sam, brez pomoči ali družbe: He lives by himself. (= on his own)
  • each other / one another = drug drugega: They love each other (ne themselves, kar bi pomenilo »vsak sebe«).
Pazi: nekateri glagoli v angleščini niso povratni, čeprav so v slovenščini: relax, feel, concentrate, meet, wash, dress, shave, hurry, remember. I feel tired (ne I feel myself tired), We met at school, Hurry up!

Besede na -ever in nedoločni zaimki

BesedaPomenPrimer
whoeverkdorkoliWhoever finishes first wins.
whateverkarkoliDo whatever you want.
whereverkjerkoli, kamorkoliSit wherever you like.
wheneverkadarkoliCall me whenever you need help.
howeverkakorkoli, še takoHowever hard I try, I can't do it.
some- / any- / no- / every- + body, thing, wherenekdo, kdorkoli, nihče, vsiIs anybody there? Nobody knows. Everything is ready.

some- v trdilnih povedih in ponudbah (Would you like something to drink?), any- v vprašanjih in nikalnicah ter s pomenom »katerikoli« (You can ask anybody), no- z glagolom v trdilni obliki (I saw nobody = I didn't see anybody).

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 4: Vstavi pravi zaimek

Naloga

Fill in the gap: The two sisters don't get on. They never talk to ___. / Be careful with that knife or you'll hurt ___.

Kako prepoznaš: vrzel je na mestu predmeta, osebek je ista oseba ali sta dve osebi, ki delata nekaj druga drugi.

  • „fill in the gap“
  • „-self / -selves“
  • „each other“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Ugotovi, kdo dela in komu.

    Sestri se pogovarjata druga z drugo; ti boš poškodoval sebe.

  2. Vzajemno dejanje → each other; ista oseba → povratni zaimek, ki se ujema z osebkom.

    each other; you → yourself.

  3. Preveri, da glagol v angleščini res potrebuje zaimek (ne pri relax, feel, meet).

    They never talk to each other. … or you'll hurt yourself.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: Nobody helped us; we built the treehouse ___.

Poudarjalno: ourselves.

Primer 2: You can invite ___ you like to the party.

»kogarkoli«: whoever (ali anyone).

Ključ: kateri tip naloge je to?

Poišči besedo iz navodila naloge in skoči na pravi postopek.

V navodilu ali povedi piše …Tip
„ask a question“, „underlined“Tip 1 · Postavi vprašanje za podčrtani del
„question tag“, poved se konča z „, ___?“Tip 2 · Vprašalni dodatek
„Could you tell me“, „Do you know“, „I wonder“, „more polite“Tip 3 · Posredno vprašanje

Pogoste napake

  • Manjkajoči pomožni glagol: Where you live? → Where do you live?
  • -s ali pretekla oblika za does/did: Does she works? Did you went? → Does she work? Did you go?
  • did v vprašanju po osebku: Who did break the vase? → Who broke the vase?
  • Obrnjen besedni red v posrednem vprašanju: Can you tell me where is the bank? → where the bank is.
  • Napačen dodatek pri I am: amn't I → aren't I.
  • themselves namesto each other pri vzajemnem dejanju.
  • Povratni zaimek pri glagolih, ki ga ne potrebujejo: I relaxed myself → I relaxed.
