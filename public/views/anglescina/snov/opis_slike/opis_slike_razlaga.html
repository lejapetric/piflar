Kako opišeš sliko

Štirje koraki, ki jih uporabiš pri vsaki sliki.

Razlaga

Pri prvi nalogi ustnega izpita dobiš fotografijo in kratko poved ali vprašanje. Slika je le izhodišče za pogovor: komisija ne ocenjuje, kako natančno našteješ predmete, ampak ali znaš o prizoru govoriti, ugibati, ga povezati s temo in povedati svoje mnenje. Opis naj traja približno eno minuto (8 do 12 povedi), potem sledijo vprašanja.

  1. Opiši (2–3 povedi): kje je prizor, kdo je na sliki in kaj počne. Uporabljaj present continuous: A girl is drawing.
  2. Ugibaj (2 povedi): kaj se je zgodilo prej, kako se ljudje počutijo, kdo so. Uporabi seem, might, must, looks as if. To loči oceno 3 od ocene 5.
  3. Poveži s temo (1 poved): The picture is about… Tako preideš od slike k iztočnici.
  4. Mnenje (3–4 povedi): odgovori na iztočnico, navedi razlog in primer iz svojega življenja, lahko tudi drugo plat.
Pazi: ne naštevaj (There is a tree. There is a dog. There is a car.). Izberi tri ali štiri stvari, ki so pomembne za temo, in jih poveži v povedi z and, while, so, because.

Univerzalni postopek

  1. Med pripravo (15 minut za ves listek) si zapiši 5 do 8 ključnih besed za sliko in eno stališče do iztočnice. Ne piši celih povedi.

    park, guitar, sketchbook, phone; relaxed, friends after school; screens vs hobbies → both

  2. Opiši: kraj, ljudje, dejanja.

    The picture shows a group of teenagers sitting on the grass in a park. In the foreground two boys are playing the guitar…

  3. Ugibaj o tem, česar na sliki ne vidiš.

    It looks like a warm summer afternoon… I think they might be friends who got together after school.

  4. Poveži s temo in odgovori na iztočnico z razlogom in primerom.

    In my opinion, most teenagers enjoy both: we use phones to keep in touch, but we also need hobbies…

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: Slaba začetna poved: There are people. There is grass. There is a guitar.

Boljša: A group of friends is relaxing on the grass, and two of them are playing the guitar. Ena poved, trije podatki, glagol v present continuous.

Primer 2: Ugibanje brez modalnih glagolov: They are friends.

Z ugibanjem: They must be good friends, because they look very relaxed together.

Kako odgovarjaš izpraševalcu

Pogovor po opisu slike in vodeni pogovor o temi.

Razlaga

Po opisu izpraševalec postavi tri do pet vprašanj, ki gredo od slike k tvojemu življenju in nato k splošni temi. Pri merilu sodelovanje v pogovoru dobiš točke, če odgovarjaš razvito in sam vodiš pogovor naprej. Odgovor z enim stavkom (Yes, I do.) prinese malo točk, tudi če je pravilen.

Pravilo O-R-P: odgovor, razlog, primer

Vsak odgovor naj ima tri dele: odgovor (Yes, definitely.), razlog (because it helps me relax) in primer ali osebno izkušnjo (Last summer, for example…). Dve do štiri povedi so dovolj; nato počakaj na naslednje vprašanje.

Pazi: če ne poznaš besede, ne preklopi v slovenščino in ne utihni. Opiši jo (the thing you use to…). Opisovanje je jezikovna spretnost, ki jo komisija ceni.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 2: Vprašanje izpraševalca

Naloga

Do you think hobbies are important? Why?

Kako prepoznaš: izpraševalec vpraša po tvojih izkušnjah, mnenju ali o splošni temi. Vprašanja z Do you…? Is it…? niso vprašanja z da ali ne, ampak povabilo k razlagi.

  • „What do you think…?“
  • „Why…?“
  • „Should…?“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Odgovori neposredno v prvi povedi.

    Yes, definitely.

  2. Dodaj razlog z because.

    A hobby gives you something to look forward to and teaches you skills that school doesn't…

  3. Dodaj primer ali osebno izkušnjo.

    …for example patience if you play an instrument.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: Do you like reading? Slabo: Yes.

Dobro: Yes, I do, especially fantasy, because it takes me to another world. The last book I read was…

Primer 2: Should schools ban phones? Ne veš, kaj misliš? It's hard to say. On the one hand…, on the other hand…, so maybe phones should be allowed only during breaks.

Tip 3: Besedilna iztočnica

Naloga

Read the text. You will not be asked to retell it; it will only serve as a basis for discussion.

Besedilo je kratko pismo bralca, oglas ali odlomek članka, npr. pismo dijaka, ki se pritožuje, da šola prepoveduje telefone.

Kako prepoznaš: namesto slike je na listku kratko besedilo. Navodilo izrecno pove, da ga ne obnavljaš.

  • „Read the text“
  • „not be asked to retell it“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Ugotovi, kdo piše, o čem in s kakšnim stališčem.

    The writer is a student who thinks the phone ban is unfair.

  2. V eni povedi povej problem s svojimi besedami (ne beri iz besedila).

    The text is about whether schools should ban mobile phones.

  3. Povej svoje stališče in ga utemelji kot pri sliki.

    I partly agree with the writer, because… However…

Young people today don't know how to spend their free time without a screen.

Več besed: Besedišče, Prosti čas in hobiji.

Uporabne povedi za to sliko

  • They seem to be having a great time.
  • It looks like a warm summer afternoon.
  • What strikes me is that only one of them is on his phone.

Vzorčni opis (približno 1 minuta)

The picture shows a group of teenagers sitting on the grass in a park. In the foreground two boys are playing the guitar, and a girl next to them is drawing in a sketchbook. In the background there is a boy who is looking at his phone. It looks like a warm summer afternoon, and they seem to be relaxed and happy. I think they might be friends who got together after school. The picture is connected to how young people spend their free time. Some people say we can't live without screens, but this photo shows the opposite. In my opinion, most teenagers enjoy both: we use phones to keep in touch, but we also need hobbies like music or sport, because they help us unwind and meet people face to face.

Vprašanja izpraševalca in vzorčni odgovori

  1. What do you usually do in your free time?

    I usually play basketball with my friends twice a week, because it's the best way for me to relax after school. At weekends I also like reading fantasy novels.

  2. Do you think hobbies are important? Why?

    Yes, definitely. A hobby gives you something to look forward to and teaches you skills that school doesn't, for example patience if you play an instrument.

  3. Is it better to spend free time alone or with others?

    It depends on the person. I need both: time alone to recharge, for instance when I read, and time with friends, because that's when I have the most fun.

  4. How has free time changed since your parents were young?

    My parents spent much more time outdoors and met friends without arranging it by phone. Today we have more choice, but we also spend more time indoors in front of screens.

Višja raven Tako zveni odgovor ravni C1:

Far from being glued to their screens, the teenagers in the picture appear to be absorbed in creative pursuits. Were schools to offer more extracurricular activities, fewer young people would drift into passive entertainment.

Vzorčni opis (približno 1 minuta)

In this photo a teenage girl is sitting in her bedroom and recording a video with her phone, which is on a small stand. There is a laptop on the desk and some posters on the wall behind her. On the one hand, social media help us stay in touch, share our ideas and even learn new things. On the other hand, they can create a lot of peer pressure, because everybody shows only the perfect side of their life. So I would say social media can make our lives better, but only if we use them wisely and protect our privacy.

Vprašanja izpraševalca in vzorčni odgovori

  1. Which social networks do you use and what for?

    I mostly use Instagram to follow my friends and YouTube for tutorials. For example, I learned how to edit photos from YouTube videos.

  2. Why do so many teenagers want to become influencers?

    I think it looks like an easy way to become famous and earn money. But most people don't see how much work and pressure there is behind every post.

  3. How can social media affect self-esteem?

    When you constantly compare yourself with perfect photos, you can start to feel that you're not good enough. That's why I think we should remember that most pictures are edited.

  4. Should parents control what their children post online?

    For younger children, yes, because they don't understand the risks. Teenagers should rather be taught how to protect their privacy, because strict control usually doesn't work.

  2. Why do many teenagers skip breakfast?

    Mostly because they get up late and are in a hurry. Some also think it helps them lose weight, which isn't true, because then they eat more junk food later.

  3. Should schools do more for students' health?

    Yes. Schools could offer healthier meals and more sports lessons, because many students don't exercise at all outside school.

  4. How does stress influence our health?

    Stress can cause headaches and sleeping problems. Before exams, for example, I sleep badly and catch colds more easily.
