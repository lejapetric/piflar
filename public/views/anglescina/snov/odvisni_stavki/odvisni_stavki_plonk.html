Časovni, namerni, posledični in dopustni stavki

Cela razlaga

1Časovni stavki

when, while, as soon as, until, before, after
za veznikom sedanjik za prihodnost
I'll call you when I arrive.
by the time
do trenutka
By the time we got there, it had ended.

2Namen in posledica

to, in order to, so as to
namen (nedoločnik)
I went out to buy bread.
so that + can/could
namen (stavek)
I left early so that I could catch the bus.
so … that, such … that
posledica
It was so cold that we stayed in.

3Dopustnost in nasprotje

although, even though + stavek
čeprav
Although it rained, we went out.
despite, in spite of + -ing/samostalnik
kljub
Despite the rain, we went out.
however, nevertheless
vendar (na začetku povedi, vejica)
It was late. However, she stayed.
whereas, while
nasprotje
He likes tea, whereas she prefers coffee.

!Pazi

  • Despite brez »of«, in spite of z »of«.
