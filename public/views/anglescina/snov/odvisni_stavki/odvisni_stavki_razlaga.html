Časovni, namerni, posledični in dopustni stavki

Ti stavki povedo, kdaj, zakaj, s kakšno posledico ali kljub čemu se nekaj zgodi. Na maturi so v vrzelih pole 1B (manjka veznik ali glagolska oblika), predvsem pa v esejih, kjer z although, despite, so that, as a result povežeš misli v logično celoto.

3Podpoglavja
3Tipi nalog
~11 minBranja

Kar moraš znati

  • za when, as soon as, until, before, after, by the time, once o prihodnosti: present simple ali present perfect, nikoli will
  • namen: to / in order to / so as to + glagol; so that + osebek + can / could / will / would
  • in case = za vsak primer (previdnost), ni isto kot if
  • posledica: so + pridevnik + that, such (a) + samostalnik + that; therefore, as a result, consequently
  • dopustnost: although / even though / though + stavek; despite / in spite of + samostalnik ali -ing
  • however in nevertheless začneta novo poved ali stojita med podpičjem in vejico

Formula: za veznikom (although, so that, when) pride cel stavek z osebkom in glagolom; za predlogom (despite, because of, during) pa samostalnik ali -ing.

Ni will za časovnim veznikom

Ko govorimo o prihodnosti, je glavni stavek v prihodnjiku, časovni stavek pa v sedanjiku:

  • I will call you when I get home. (ne when I will get)
  • We won't start until everyone is here.
  • Present perfect poudari, da bo dejanje prej končano: You can go out after you have finished your homework.

Časi v preteklosti

when + past simple pri zaporednih dejanjih (When he saw me, he waved); while + past continuous za ozadje (While we were walking, it started to rain); by the time + past simple v časovnem stavku in past perfect v glavnem (By the time the police came, the thief had escaped).

Pazi: until že vsebuje »ne«: Wait until he comes = počakaj, dokler ne pride. Za rok uporabiš by (Send it by Friday = najkasneje v petek), za trajanje until (The shop is open until 8).

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 1: Glagolska oblika v časovnem stavku

Naloga

Complete with the correct form of the verbs: As soon as the results ___ (come) out, I ___ (let) you know.

Kako prepoznaš: poved ima časovni veznik in dva glagola v oklepaju, govori o prihodnosti ali o zaporedju v preteklosti.

  • „when / as soon as / until“
  • „by the time“
  • „correct form“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Poišči časovni veznik in ga označi; za njim je odvisni stavek.

    As soon as the results ___ (come) out je časovni stavek.

  2. Ugotovi čas celotne povedi.

    Rezultati še niso znani → prihodnost.

  3. Prihodnost: časovni stavek present simple (ali present perfect), glavni will. Preteklost z by the time: past perfect v glavnem stavku.

    As soon as the results come out, I will let you know.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: By the time I ___ (wake) up, everyone ___ (leave).

woke, had left.

Primer 2: I won't go to bed until I ___ (finish) this chapter.

finish ali have finished.

Namen in posledica

Zakaj nekaj naredimo in kaj iz tega sledi.

Razlaga

Namen (purpose)

IzrazZgradbaPrimer
to / in order to / so as to+ osnovna oblika (isti osebek)She went to London to improve her English.
in order not to / so as not tonikalni namenHe left early so as not to miss the bus.
so that+ osebek + can/could/will/wouldI'll write it down so that I don't forget. / She spoke slowly so that we could understand.
for+ samostalnik ali -ing (namen predmeta)We went out for a walk. This knife is for cutting bread.
in case+ stavek (previdnost)Take a jacket in case it gets cold.
Pazi: namen osebe nikoli ne izraziš s for + glagol: I came here for to learn / for learn → I came here to learn. Ko se osebka razlikujeta, uporabiš so that: I lent him my notes so that he could study.

Posledica (result)

IzrazMestoPrimer
soveznik v isti povedi (za vejico)It was raining, so we stayed in.
so + pridevnik + thattako … daThe test was so difficult that nobody finished it.
such (a) + pridevnik + samostalnik + thattako … daIt was such a difficult test that nobody finished it.
therefore, as a result, consequently, thusformalno; nova poved ali za podpičjemPrices rose. As a result, sales fell.
as a result of / due to / because of+ samostalnik (vzrok)The match was cancelled due to the storm.

Za vzrok uporabiš because, as, since + stavek ali because of, due to, owing to + samostalnik: We stayed in because it was raining = because of the rain.

Tip 2: Izberi ali vstavi pravi veznik

Naloga

Choose the correct linker: I set two alarms (so that / in case / because of) I wouldn't oversleep.

Kako prepoznaš: manjka beseda, ki poveže dva dela povedi; ponujeni so vezniki in predlogi s podobnim pomenom.

  • „linker“
  • „connector“
  • „choose the correct word“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Ugotovi logično zvezo med deloma: čas, namen, posledica, vzrok, nasprotje.

    Zakaj dva budilnika? Da ne bi zaspal → namen.

  2. Poglej, kaj sledi vrzeli: stavek (osebek + glagol), samostalnik ali -ing, osnovna oblika glagola.

    I wouldn't oversleep → cel stavek z would.

  3. Izberi izraz, ki ustreza pomenu in zgradbi.

    Namen + stavek z would → so that.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: The flight was delayed (because / because of) fog.

fog je samostalnik → because of.

Primer 2: Bring some cash (so that / in case) the card machine doesn't work.

Previdnost → in case.

Dopustnost in nasprotje

Although, despite, however, whereas.

Razlaga

IzrazKaj slediPrimer
although / even though / thoughstavek (osebek + glagol)Although it was late, we went for a walk.
despite / in spite ofsamostalnik ali -ingDespite the rain, we went out. In spite of feeling ill, she came.
despite the fact thatstavekDespite the fact that he was tired, he kept working.
however / nevertheless / nonethelessnova poved, vejicaThe hotel was cheap. However, it was very clean.
but / yetveznik v isti povediIt was expensive, but worth it.
whereas / whileprimerjava dveh nasprotijMy sister loves sport, whereas I prefer reading.
on the other handdruga plat argumentaCities offer jobs. On the other hand, they are noisy.
pridevnik + as/though + osebek + glagol (VR)čeprav še takoTired as she was, she finished the essay.
Pazi: despite nikoli nima of (despite of je napaka), in spite of pa ga vedno ima. Although in but ne uporabiš v isti povedi: Although it rained, but we went → Although it rained, we went. However ne poveže dveh stavkov z vejico; pred njim je pika ali podpičje.

Pretvorbe, ki se pogosto pojavijo

  • Although he was injured, he finished the race. = Despite being injured, he finished the race. = Despite his injury, …
  • Although the weather was bad, … = In spite of the bad weather, …
  • It was cold, but we swam. = Even though it was cold, we swam.

Tipi nalog v tem sklopu

Tip 3: Pretvorba med veznikom in predlogom

Naloga

Complete the second sentence so that it means the same: Although she had a headache, she went to work. → Despite ___, she went to work.

Kako prepoznaš: prva poved ima veznik (although, because, so that), druga se začne s predlogom (despite, because of) ali obratno.

  • „means the same“
  • „despite / in spite of“
  • „because of“
Univerzalni postopek

  1. Ugotovi, ali je nov izraz veznik (sledi stavek) ali predlog (sledi samostalnik ali -ing).

    Despite je predlog → samostalnik ali -ing.

  2. Stavek spremeni v -ing (isti osebek) ali v samostalnik (the rain, his injury), ali uporabi the fact that.

    she had a headache → having a headache ali her headache.

  3. Preveri, da se pomen ni spremenil in da ni odvečnega but ali of.

    Despite having a headache, she went to work.

Še nekaj primerov

Primer 1: The game was cancelled because it was snowing heavily. → The game was cancelled because of ___.

because of the heavy snow.

Primer 2: In spite of the high price, they bought the car. → Although ___.

Although the price was high, they bought the car.

Ključ: kateri tip naloge je to?

Poišči besedo iz navodila naloge in skoči na pravi postopek.

V navodilu ali povedi piše …Tip
„when / as soon as / until / by the time“ + glagol v oklepajuTip 1 · Glagolska oblika v časovnem stavku
„linker“, „connector“, izbira med veznikiTip 2 · Izberi ali vstavi pravi veznik
„means the same“, „despite“, „in spite of“, „because of“Tip 3 · Pretvorba med veznikom in predlogom

Pogoste napake

  • will za časovnim veznikom: when I will finish → when I finish.
  • despite of → despite ali in spite of.
  • despite + stavek: despite it was raining → despite the rain / although it was raining.
  • for + glagol za namen: I went to the shop for buy milk → to buy milk.
  • Although …, but … v isti povedi.
  • However kot veznik z vejico: It was cheap, however it was good → It was cheap. However, it was good.
  • in case in if zamenjana: in case pomeni ukrep vnaprej.
